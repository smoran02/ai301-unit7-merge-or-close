# Merge or Close

A team review game for CodePath AI301, unit 7.

**Play it here: https://smoran02.github.io/ai301-unit7-merge-or-close/**

Ren, the lead maintainer of playlistr (a made-up Python library), is away, and your team covers the pull request queue. Merge the PRs that follow the house rules and close the ones that don't.

## How it runs

The game is split into rounds called days. A day is one round of the game, not a real day, and the whole game fits in your breakout session.

- **Days 1 to 3:** review the queue. Each day adds new house rules and gives you 8 or 9 minutes for its PRs.
- **Day 4:** Ren is back, and you open a PR of your own.
- **Overtime:** four more PRs, right after day 4. Each right call adds a bonus point to your score, and a wrong one costs nothing. Short on time? Skip to your results.
- **The boss PR:** finished overtime? Take on one giant PR that breaks every house rule somewhere. It's just for fun.

One person shares the game in your breakout room and clicks for the team. Everyone else can open the same link and choose **Read along** to read the PRs on their own screen.
